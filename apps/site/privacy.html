P Privacy Policy

The short version: your machine is the default.

StackSpirit is local-first. If you use the desktop app or connect your own Linux box, your code, sessions and keys stay on machines you control. This policy explains the narrow set of things we process when you choose a cloud surface, and what we deliberately never store.

1. The short version

  • Local mode is local. The desktop app and CLI can run without a StackSpirit account. Your code, prompts, outputs, server profiles and keys stay on your devices.
  • Bring your own box stays yours. When you connect your own VPS, laptop or home server over SSH, StackSpirit Cloud is not in the data path.
  • The cloud is optional. It exists for cross-device Web App access and for Managed Homes. We collect the account and operational metadata needed to run those services.
  • No secret values in the cloud. StackSpirit Cloud never stores SSH private keys or provider OAuth tokens. Provider API keys live in your OS keychain or in the secret vault on your own Home.
  • No trackers, no ad tech, no selling data. The marketing site loads no third-party scripts, fonts or analytics.

2. Who we are

StackSpirit is an independent, MIT-licensed software project. In this policy, “StackSpirit”, “we”, “us” and “our” mean the people who maintain the project and, if you use them, operate StackSpirit Cloud and the Managed Always-On Homes service.

For privacy questions, anything in this policy, or a data request, write to syver@stackspirit.io. That address is also the contact for billing and account questions.

3. Local-first by default

The normal way to use StackSpirit is on a machine you control. There is no account gate, no license key and no trial timer. In local and SSH mode, the desktop app stores server profiles, connection details and its local database on your device. The Home stores its own database, session logs and project state.

When you do not sign in, we do not receive your code, prompt text, model output, shell history, SSH credentials or provider keys. Diagnostics you choose to export are yours to review before you send them anywhere; do not send secret values in a support message.

4. When StackSpirit Cloud is involved

An optional free account is used for cross-device sign-in to the Web App and for Managed Homes. If you create one, we process:

  • Account data: your email address, display name, authentication state and security-event records needed to keep the account safe.
  • Device and server metadata: labels you choose, host names, ports, connection status, the server fingerprint you confirmed, and timestamps. We do not receive the private half of your SSH key.
  • Job and session metadata: project names, session titles, job status, queue position, capacity numbers and timing. The content of prompts and outputs stays on the Home unless you explicitly send it through a cloud surface.
  • Billing records: plan, invoice status and the last four digits or equivalent non-sensitive identifiers returned by our payment processor. Card data is handled by Stripe, not by us.
  • Support messages: what you send to us and what we send back.

We use this data to provide the service, secure it, support you, bill correctly and understand aggregate reliability. We do not sell it, rent it or use it for advertising.

5. What we never store

StackSpirit Cloud does not store SSH private keys or provider OAuth tokens. It also does not store provider API keys as plaintext.

The desktop app verifies a server's host-key fingerprint before installing its own key. Server profiles keep a path to a key, not the key material. Provider secrets live in the local OS keychain or in the secret vault on the Home. The AI only sees secret names and metadata, never values.

If you enable a cloud feature that must hold a session token for you, we treat it as a short-lived credential, scope it to that feature and expire it. We do not use it to read your repository or your provider account.

6. Managed Homes

A Managed Always-On Home is a dedicated Contabo VPS provisioned on your behalf. We receive machine health and provisioning metadata so we can operate it. Your code, projects and session data on that Home remain yours. We do not browse your workspace or read your prompts for our own purposes.

Off-site encrypted R2 backups are optional and off by default. If you enable them, StackSpirit writes an encrypted copy of your ~/.stackspirit data to Cloudflare R2. The add-on is billed monthly and you can turn it off.

Managed Homes are paid hardware, not a software license. Billing is handled by Stripe; current prices, the one-time setup fee where applicable, VAT treatment and the +20% StackSpirit markup over Contabo's regular rate are shown before checkout. See the Terms of Service for billing and cancellation.

7. Subprocessors

We keep the list short and use providers that process data on our instructions:

  • Cloudflare — website hosting and DNS, security in front of the site and API, and Cloudflare R2 for optional encrypted backups.
  • Stripe — payment processing, invoices and billing records for Managed Homes and the backup add-on.
  • Resend — transactional email such as sign-in, receipts and operational notices.
  • Contabo — the underlying VPS infrastructure for Managed Homes.

Each provider has its own privacy terms. We do not add advertising, analytics or session-recording subprocessors. If this list changes in a material way, we will update this page.

8. Cookies and analytics

The marketing site at stackspirit.io sets no cookies, uses no localStorage and loads no third-party trackers, analytics or advertising code. The optional plan catalog fetch goes only to api.stackspirit.io.

The Web App uses the minimum cookies or local storage needed to keep you signed in and protect the session. Cloudflare may process technical request logs and security signals as part of hosting. We do not use those logs to build an advertising profile.

9. Retention and deletion

Local data stays on your devices until you delete it. Cloud account and operational records are kept while your account or Managed Home is active, then for a short period needed to close billing, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations. Backup copies age out under the provider's retention windows.

You can ask us to delete your account by emailing syver@stackspirit.io. We will confirm what is deleted and what must be retained for legal or accounting reasons. Deleting a Managed Home destroys the machine and its local data; export anything you need first.

10. Security

We use TLS in transit, least-privilege access, pinned SSH host keys and a secret-by-name design. The open-source security model and its limits are documented in SECURITY.md. No system is perfectly secure; if you find a vulnerability, email syver@stackspirit.io instead of posting secret details in a public issue.

11. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete personal data we hold, to object to some processing, or to complain to your local data-protection authority. To make a request, email syver@stackspirit.io. We may need to verify that the request comes from the account holder.

You can avoid the cloud entirely by using local mode. You can remove a saved server, revoke its key and delete local data from the desktop app.

12. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the product or the law changes. The “last updated” date at the top is the reliable version marker. If a change materially affects what we collect or why, we will make that clear on the site or by email before it applies to existing accounts.

13. Contact

Questions, corrections and data requests: syver@stackspirit.io. We are a small independent project, so please allow a reasonable amount of time for a personal reply.
